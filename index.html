Context

The business problem

SwiftBasket is a quick-commerce company. Orders are growing, but profit is not growing in the same way.

The COO wants to know which growth is worth keeping. She suspects busy customers and fast-growing stores lose money through discounts, late deliveries, refunds and stock-outs.

How we measure it: profit per order

Gross margin+Delivery fee− Discount−Picking cost− Delivery cost−Refund=Profit per order

One number for every order, so growth is judged by profit, not by order count.
